AI-assisted conversation / Writing tool

Cuewise

A conversation reflection and reply-coaching application that helps users separate literal wording from assumptions and consider thoughtful ways to respond.

Open CuewiseView source code

The problem being explored

It can be difficult to interpret a short message without adding assumptions about the sender's intentions. Cuewise explores a more careful reflection process that distinguishes what was actually written from possible interpretations.

Product workflow

  • Provide a conversation excerpt or screenshot.
  • Extract text from supported screenshots.
  • Review alternative interpretations rather than a single definitive conclusion.
  • Choose a communication goal and edit suggested replies.

Engineering focus

The project combines browser-based text extraction, interaction design and optional AI-assisted analysis. The interface is designed to keep suggested responses editable and make uncertainty visible.

Important limitation

Cuewise cannot read minds or prove another person's feelings, intentions or meaning. Its output is a reflection aid, not evidence about what someone else thinks.
