Commerce platform / Business operating system

QuickCart

A Nigeria-first business and shopping platform connecting independent sellers with buyers, with a broader operating-system vision for the work behind each sale.

Open QuickCartView source code

The problem being explored

Independent sellers may manage storefronts, customers, orders, stock, money, fulfilment and marketing across disconnected tools. QuickCart is intended to connect the buyer journey with practical seller operations without assuming every business operates like a large retailer.

Current product foundation

  • Shareable customer storefronts and product catalogues.
  • Seller order management, including manual order entry.
  • Customer checkout and order-status tracking.
  • Seller-triggered WhatsApp handoff and message drafting.
  • Early Business OS foundations for expenses, invoices, cashflow summaries, stock movements, suppliers, purchase orders, delivery assignments and rules-based recommendations.

Full Business OS direction

  • Customer management and connected seller workflows.
  • Reliable finance ledger, invoice lifecycle and reconciliation.
  • Inventory history, supplier management and purchase-order fulfilment.
  • Delivery assignment, tracking history and proof of fulfilment.
  • Marketing attribution, referrals and abandoned-checkout recovery.
  • Explainable seller recommendations with explicit approval and audit history.

Engineering focus

The project is built as a full-stack web application, with a React frontend and a Node.js, Express and MongoDB backend. The engineering work focuses on connecting seller and customer workflows, maintaining clear ownership of business data, and making the experience usable on mobile devices.

Current stage and limitations

The current application is an early product, not yet the complete Business OS. Finance and inventory ledger guarantees, verified payment-sandbox evidence, delivery proof, marketing conversion workflows, audited seller-approved actions, comprehensive tenant-isolation tests and full production E2E evidence remain release gates. Payments are arranged directly between buyers and sellers; QuickCart does not hold funds or provide escrow. WhatsApp handoff is seller-triggered and is not proof of automatic message delivery. Real-world seller validation is still needed.
