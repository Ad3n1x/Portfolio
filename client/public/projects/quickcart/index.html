QuickCart

A Nigeria-first commerce application designed to help small businesses manage a shareable storefront and the work that happens after a customer places an order.

Open QuickCartView source code

The problem being explored

Small sellers may manage product information, customer messages, orders and delivery updates across separate tools. QuickCart explores how those tasks can be brought into a connected workflow without assuming every seller operates like a large retailer.

What the product includes

  • Shareable customer-facing storefronts and product catalogues.
  • Seller workflows for creating and managing orders, including manual order entry.
  • Customer-facing checkout and order-status tracking.
  • Delivery updates and WhatsApp-oriented customer communication.
  • Business-facing views for managing products and day-to-day selling activity.

Engineering focus

The project is built as a full-stack web application, with a React frontend and a Node.js, Express and MongoDB backend. The engineering work focuses on connecting seller and customer workflows, maintaining clear ownership of business data, and making the experience usable on mobile devices.

Current stage and limitations

QuickCart is an active independent software project. Its core workflows have been developed, but broad adoption and real-world seller outcomes have not been established. This page does not claim verified revenue, active seller counts or commercial traction.
