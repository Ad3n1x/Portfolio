UNI-TRACK

A user-controlled productivity and tracking platform for habits, goals, tasks, activities and personal metrics, with a focus on useful progress views and data control.

Open UNI-TRACKView source code

The problem being explored

Personal plans and recurring activities can become scattered across notes, reminders and separate tools. UNI-TRACK explores how people can organise those activities and review progress in one coherent digital workspace.

Product areas

  • Organisation of goals, habits and tasks.
  • Activity and progress tracking.
  • Views intended to make ongoing work easier to review.
  • A responsive web experience designed around practical everyday use.

Engineering focus

The project explores account security, user-owned records, tracker encryption, export and practical data handling. Privacy properties must be evaluated against actual data flows, not inferred from the product description.

Current privacy and recovery boundary

The current mainline stores compatible non-extractable private keys in browser IndexedDB. A user-facing private-key recovery/export flow is not yet verified on the main frontend, and the current separate lv3node backend does not yet expose the recovery-envelope endpoints expected by the newer frontend candidate. Clearing browser data or losing the device may make encrypted records inaccessible. Do not describe UNI-TRACK as universally end-to-end encrypted or fully recoverable until the frontend and backend recovery workflow is implemented and tested together.
